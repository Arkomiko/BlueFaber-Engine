# Editable node templates

[English documentation](README.md)

BLUSTREE and BLUSMOR are linked packages of editable nodes, assets and profile requirements. A developer can insert either graph into a project, inspect and change its internals, rename the local namespace, and verify that resource references survive saving and reopening.

| Package | Node groups |
| --- | --- |
| **BLUSTREE** | Logs, leaves and saplings. Planks and building blocks. Doors, trapdoors and fences. Wood tools. Boats and wooden utility items. |
| **BLUSMOR** | Ore, ingots and nuggets. Weapons and tools. Armor and horse armor. Construction and metal variants. Boats, minecarts, rails and mechanisms. Buckets, trident and lightning rod. |

The first BLUSTREE slice is a log, planks, and a recipe. The first BLUSMOR slice is an ore-to-ingot flow. Build the small slices first, record expected behavior in-game, and extend or port one subsystem at a time.
